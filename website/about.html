Our project

BugBucket is an AI-powered bug classification website. It reads a software bug report and predicts which area of the product it belongs to, such as User Interface, Networking, or Security and Privacy.

Our purpose: help developers and testers sort bugs faster, so each bug reaches the right team sooner.

Good to know

  • Categories come from the Bugzilla component where each bug was reported, not from the type of bug.
  • The model learned from short bug titles, so brief and clear descriptions work best.
  • Results are suggestions to help sorting, not final decisions.

FAQ

Why is the match score sometimes low?

The report may be vague or fit several areas. Add details such as what you did and what went wrong.

Where does the data come from?

BugRepo (EASE 2025), a collection of Mozilla Bugzilla bugs, licensed CC BY 4.0, doi:10.5281/zenodo.15004067.

How accurate is it?

83% on 11,318 bugs the model had never seen (macro F1 0.80). Performance is the hardest category.

Does it store my bug report?

No. The text is only used to make the prediction and is not saved.
